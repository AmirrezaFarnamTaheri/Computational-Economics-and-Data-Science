Section 03 • Matter.js Market Lab

Decentralized Bids, Asks & Price Discovery

Buyer and seller particles collide in a bounded market. Each collision updates a stylized transaction price toward the midpoint of a buyer bid and seller ask, making convergence an observable process rather than a static supply-demand diagram. The simulation is pedagogical: it illustrates decentralized price discovery, not a calibrated microstructure model.

3. Aiyagari General Equilibrium Algorithm

Nested fixed-point bisection on aggregate capital interest rate $r$ and household distribution $\mu(a, z)$.

flowchart TD
    guess["Guess r(0)"] --> vfi["Solve Bellman V(a,z; r)"]
    vfi --> policy["Extract Policy a'(a,z)"]
    policy --> dist["Invariant Distribution mu(a,z)"]
    dist --> agg["Aggregate Supply K_s(r)"]
    agg --> clear{"|K_s - K_d| < eps?"}
    clear -->|No| update["Update r(k+1) via Bisection"]
    update --> vfi
    clear -->|Yes| eq["Equilibrium (r*, w*, mu*)"]
    classDef outer fill:#EFF6FF,stroke:#3B82F6,stroke-width:1.8px,color:#1E3A8A;
