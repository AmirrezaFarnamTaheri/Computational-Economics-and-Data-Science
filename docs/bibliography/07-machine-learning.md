# Machine Learning — Bibliography

> Pathway in one line: prediction as an estimation problem — with its own biases to manage.

## Reading pathway

1. Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman — the statistical core (ESL).
2. Shalev-Shwartz & Ben-David — learning theory without hand-waving.
3. Breiman (2001) + Friedman (2001) — forests and boosting, the tabular workhorses.
4. Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville — deep learning's engineering canon.
5. Athey & Imbens (2019) — what economists should borrow first.

## Seminal

- **Rosenblatt (1958).** *The Perceptron: A Probabilistic Model...*
  Psychological Review. — Neural networks, attempt one; the hype-and-winter
  cycle it started is a lesson itself.
- **Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams (1986).** *Learning Representations by
  Back-Propagating Errors.* Nature. — Backpropagation; the training engine of
  lecture 06.
- **Breiman (2001).** *Random Forests* (Machine Learning) and *Statistical
  Modeling: The Two Cultures* (Statistical Science). — Ensemble prediction and
  the culture clash framing this module's opening lecture.
- **Friedman (2001).** *Greedy Function Approximation: A Gradient Boosting
  Machine.* Annals of Statistics. — Boosting as gradient descent in function
  space.
- **Vapnik (1998).** *Statistical Learning Theory.* Wiley. — VC dimension and
  generalization bounds; why regularization works.

## Modern

- **Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman (2009).** *The Elements of Statistical
  Learning*, 2nd ed. Springer. — The reference for every supervised method in
  the module.
- **Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville (2016).** *Deep Learning.* MIT Press. —
  Architecture, optimization, and representation learning.
- **Chen & Guestrin (2016).** *XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System.* KDD.
  — The implementation details (histogram splits, sparsity awareness) behind
  lecture 02.
- **Vaswani et al. (2017).** *Attention Is All You Need.* NeurIPS. —
  Transformers; the architecture of lecture 10 and everything after.
- **Athey & Imbens (2019).** *Machine Learning Methods That Economists Should
  Know About.* Annual Review of Economics. — The bridge paper for this module's
  audience.

## Theoretical

- **Shalev-Shwartz & Ben-David (2014).** *Understanding Machine Learning: From
  Theory to Algorithms.* Cambridge University Press. — PAC learning, VC
  dimension, and generalization with honest proofs.
- **Mohri, Rostamizadeh & Talwalkar (2012).** *Foundations of Machine
  Learning.* MIT Press. — Rademacher complexity and regularization theory; the
  formal companion to ESL.
