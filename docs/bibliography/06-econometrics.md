# Econometrics — Bibliography

> Pathway in one line: know exactly which assumption buys which conclusion.

## Reading pathway

1. Hayashi — the unified estimation theory (OLS → MLE → GMM).
2. Angrist & Pischke — applied causal econometrics as practiced.
3. Imbens & Rubin — potential outcomes in full depth.
4. Chernozhukov et al. (2018) — machine learning entering causal estimation.
5. Goodman-Bacon (2021) — why staggered DiD needed repair.

## Seminal

- **Frisch (1933) & Waugh (1933).** Partialing-out results (completed by
  Lovell, 1963). — The FWL theorem behind every fixed-effects and debiased
  regression.
- **White (1980).** *A Heteroskedasticity-Consistent Covariance Matrix
  Estimator...* Econometrica. — Robust standard errors; the default you use
  without thinking.
- **Heckman (1979).** *Sample Selection Bias as a Specification Error.*
  Econometrica. — Selection models; the classic correction.
- **Hansen (1982).** *Large Sample Properties of Generalized Method of Moments
  Estimators.* Econometrica. — GMM's asymptotics; one theory for IV, MLE, and
  calibration tests.
- **Arellano & Bond (1991).** *Some Tests of Specification for Panel
  Data.* Review of Economic Studies. — Difference GMM for dynamic panels.

## Modern

- **Hayashi (2000).** *Econometrics.* Princeton University Press. — Estimation
  and inference as one subject; the module's theoretical backbone.
- **Angrist & Pischke (2009).** *Mostly Harmless Econometrics.* Princeton
  University Press. — The applied causal toolkit with honesty about limits.
- **Imbens & Rubin (2015).** *Causal Inference for Statistics, Social, and
  Biomedical Sciences.* Cambridge University Press. — Potential outcomes from
  axioms to practice.
- **Chernozhukov, Chetverikov, Demirer, Duflo, Hansen, Newey & Robins (2018).**
  *Double/Debiased Machine Learning.* The Econometrics Journal. — Neyman
  orthogonality + cross-fitting; lecture 03's modern frontier.
- **Goodman-Bacon (2021).** *Difference-in-Differences with Variation in
  Treatment Timing.* QJE. — The TWFE decomposition that reshaped DiD practice.

## Theoretical

- **Davidson & MacKinnon (1993).** *Estimation and Inference in Econometrics.*
  Oxford University Press. — Asymptotic methods and bootstrap reasoning in
  depth.
