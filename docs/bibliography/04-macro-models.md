# Macro Models — Bibliography

> Pathway in one line: calibrate, solve, estimate — and know which of the three you are doing.

## Reading pathway

1. King, Plosser & Rebelo (1988) — the RBC production recipe.
2. Uhlig (1999) — log-linearization as a mechanical toolkit.
3. Ljungqvist & Sargent — recursive macro with complete treatments.
4. Fernández-Villaverde, Rubio-Ramírez & Schorfheide (2016) — estimation after
   calibration.
5. Kaplan, Moll & Violante (2018) — where the frontier moved (HANK).

## Seminal

- **Solow (1956).** *A Contribution to the Theory of Economic Growth.* QJE. —
  The growth model everything here builds on.
- **Lucas (1976).** *Econometric Policy Evaluation: A Critique.* Econometrica. —
  Why deep parameters, not reduced forms; the reason this module solves models
  instead of extrapolating them.
- **Kydland & Prescott (1982).** *Time to Build and Aggregate Fluctuations.*
  Econometrica. — Calibration as a discipline; the template for RBC labs.
- **King, Plosser & Rebelo (1988).** *Production, Growth and Business Cycles.*
  JME. — The standard RBC normalization and solution recipe used in 03A–03D.

## Modern

- **Uhlig (1999).** *A Toolkit for Analysing Nonlinear Dynamic Models Easily.*
  In: Computational Methods for the Study of Dynamic Economies. Oxford. —
  The perturbation cookbook behind the log-linearized labs.
- **Fernández-Villaverde, Rubio-Ramírez & Schorfheide (2016).** *The Solution
  and Estimation of DSGE Models.* Handbook of Macroeconomics, Vol. 2. —
  Solution accuracy and Bayesian estimation in one survey.
- **Kaplan, Moll & Violante (2018).** *Monetary Policy According to HANK.* AER.
  — Heterogeneous-agent macro with sequence-space methods; the model behind
  lecture 06's frontier.

## Theoretical

- **Stokey & Lucas (1989).** *Recursive Methods in Economic Dynamics.* Harvard
  University Press. — The macro-side applications of the DP theorems.
- **Ljungqvist & Sargent (2018).** *Recursive Macroeconomic Theory*, 4th ed.
  MIT Press. — Search, incomplete markets, and Ramsey problems with exercises
  matching this curriculum's style.
