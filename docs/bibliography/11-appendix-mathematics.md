# Mathematics Appendix — Bibliography

> Pathway in one line: the theorems behind the fixed points, expectations, and decompositions used everywhere else.

## Reading pathway

1. Rudin — real analysis in its canonical compact form.
2. Axler or Strang — linear algebra with the right emphasis.
3. Williams — probability with martingales, measure-first but friendly.
4. Luenberger — vector-space optimization (the appendix's secret weapon).
5. Billingsley — measure-theoretic probability in full.

## Seminal

- **Banach (1922).** *Sur les opérations dans les ensembles abstraits...*
  Fundamenta Mathematicae. — The contraction mapping theorem, from his
  doctoral thesis.
- **Kolmogorov (1933).** *Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung.*
  Springer. — Probability as measure theory; the axiomatization used today.

## Modern

- **Rudin (1976).** *Principles of Mathematical Analysis*, 3rd ed. McGraw-Hill.
  — The metric-space and sequence chapters are exactly this appendix's level.
- **Axler (1997).** *Linear Algebra Done Right*, 2nd ed. Springer. —
  Spectral theory without determinants; eigen-stability done cleanly.
- **Strang (2016).** *Introduction to Linear Algebra*, 5th ed. Wellesley-
  Cambridge. — The applied complement: four fundamental subspaces, SVD.
- **Williams (1991).** *Probability with Martingales.* Cambridge University
  Press. — Conditional expectation as projection, done with wit.

## Theoretical

- **Billingsley (2012).** *Probability and Measure*, anniversary ed. Wiley. —
  The full measure-probability bridge.
- **Luenberger (1969).** *Optimization by Vector Space Methods.* Wiley. —
  Duality and fixed points in Hilbert/Banach spaces; where the appendix's
  ideas earn their keep.
