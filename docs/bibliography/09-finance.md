# Finance — Bibliography

> Pathway in one line: prices as expectations under the right measure — and what estimation does to that sentence.

## Reading pathway

1. Cochrane — the SDF viewpoint this module teaches first.
2. Campbell, Lo & MacKinlay — the econometrics of the claims.
3. Shreve — stochastic calculus for the continuous-time lectures.
4. Glasserman — Monte Carlo done with error control.
5. Back or Duffie — the full general theory when you need it.

## Seminal

- **Markowitz (1952).** *Portfolio Selection.* Journal of Finance. —
  Diversification as optimization.
- **Sharpe (1964).** *Capital Asset Prices...* Journal of Finance. — The CAPM
  (with Lintner 1965, Mossin 1966).
- **Fama (1970).** *Efficient Capital Markets: A Review of Theory and
  Empirical Work.* Journal of Finance. — The EMH taxonomy the tests target.
- **Black & Scholes (1973); Merton (1973).** *The Pricing of Options and
  Corporate Liabilities* (JPE); *Theory of Rational Option Pricing* (Bell
  Journal). — No-arbitrage pricing and the continuous-hedging argument.
- **Merton (1974).** *On the Pricing of Corporate Debt...* Journal of Finance.
  — Structural credit models; equity as a call on assets.
- **Fama & French (1993).** *Common Risk Factors in the Returns on Stocks and
  Bonds.* Journal of Financial Economics. — The factor model this module
  tests.

## Modern

- **Campbell, Lo & MacKinlay (1997).** *The Econometrics of Financial Markets.*
  Princeton University Press. — Empirical methods for everything in lectures
  01–03.
- **Cochrane (2005).** *Asset Pricing*, revised ed. Princeton University
  Press. — One discount factor to organize them all.
- **Glasserman (2003).** *Monte Carlo Methods in Financial Engineering.*
  Springer. — Variance reduction, Greeks by pathwise/likelihood methods.

## Theoretical

- **Shreve (2004).** *Stochastic Calculus for Finance II.* Springer. —
  Binomial-to-continuous limits with every theorem proved.
- **Duffie (2001).** *Dynamic Asset Pricing Theory*, 3rd ed. Princeton
  University Press. — The general equilibrium-pricing framework.
- **Back (2017).** *Asset Pricing and Portfolio Choice Theory*, 2nd ed. Oxford
  University Press. — Modern, rigorous, and close to the notation used here.
