## Summary

**What problem are we solving?** Partial equilibrium analyzes one market in isolation (e.g., the market for apples). **General Equilibrium (GE)** analyzes all markets simultaneously. It asks: Can prices adjust so that *every* market clears at the same time? This framework captures the ripple effects of shocks: how a tax on steel affects the price of cars, the wages of auto workers, and the demand for housing in Detro
