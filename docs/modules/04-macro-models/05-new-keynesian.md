## Summary

**What problem are we solving?** The RBC model assumes prices adjust instantly. This implies monetary policy (printing money) has no effect on the real economy ("money neutrality"). But in the real world, central banks *do* affect output and employment. Recessions often follow monetary contractions. The **New Keynesian (NK)** model bridges this gap. It adds **nominal rigidities** (sticky prices) to the RBC framework.
